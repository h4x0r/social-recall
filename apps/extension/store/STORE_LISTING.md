## Extension Name
Social Recall

## Short Description (132 characters max)
Remember everyone you meet on LinkedIn. AI-powered insights, job change alerts, and personal notes for your professional network.

## Detailed Description

### Turn Your LinkedIn Browsing Into Relationship Intelligence

Social Recall automatically saves and analyzes LinkedIn profiles as you browse, building a personal CRM that remembers everyone you've encountered.

**Key Features:**

**Automatic Profile Capture**
Browse LinkedIn naturally. Social Recall quietly saves profile data in the background—no clicking required. Your network grows as you work.

**AI-Powered Insights**
Each contact is analyzed to identify their professional archetype (Builder, Advisor, Connector, etc.), key skills, and potential collaboration opportunities.

**Job Change Alerts**
Know when your contacts switch companies. Social Recall compares current data with what it remembered, alerting you to career moves you might have missed.

**Personal Notes**
Add private notes to any contact. Meeting notes, follow-up reminders, or context about how you know them—all saved alongside their profile.

**Profile History**
Track how contacts evolve over time. See when they changed titles, switched companies, or updated their skills.

**Beautiful Interface**
A floating panel appears on LinkedIn profiles, showing you everything you know about this person at a glance. Minimalist design that stays out of your way.

**Works Offline**
All data is stored locally in your browser. Access your network intelligence even without an internet connection.

**Optional Cloud Sync**
Sign in with Google to sync across devices and access your contacts from our web app.

---

**Who It's For:**

• **Founders** building their network as they grow their company
• **Investors** tracking portfolio company executives and deal flow
• **Salespeople** who need to remember every prospect conversation
• **Recruiters** managing relationships with candidates
• **Anyone** who wants to remember the people they meet professionally

---

**Privacy First:**

• We only read publicly visible LinkedIn profile data
• All data is stored locally by default
• No access to your LinkedIn credentials
• Cloud sync is optional and encrypted
• Export or delete your data anytime

---

**Getting Started:**

1. Install Social Recall
2. Browse to any LinkedIn profile
3. The floating panel appears with AI insights
4. Add notes and let it remember for you

Your network is your edge. Social Recall makes it queryable.
